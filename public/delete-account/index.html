Delete your account and data

You can request permanent deletion of your Personal Haven account and the personal data associated with it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us from the same Google email address you use to sign in to Personal Haven.
  2. Use the subject Delete my Personal Haven account.
  3. We may ask you to confirm account ownership. Do not send passwords, payment card details, journal content, or other sensitive information.
Request account deletion

Data that will be deleted

  • Your Personal Haven account and profile information.
  • Journal entries, moods, goals, tasks, events, and routines.
  • Wellness, food, shopping-list, and budgeting records.
  • Preferences, notification settings, and cloud-stored app content.
  • Active entitlement records associated with your account.

Data that may be retained

Records that we must keep for fraud prevention, security, tax, accounting, dispute resolution, or other legal obligations may be retained only for the period required by law. Google Play separately maintains purchase and subscription records under Google's policies. Aggregated or de-identified analytics that can no longer identify you may also remain.

Processing time

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 additional days before automatic removal. You will receive confirmation when the active account deletion is complete.

Deleting Personal Haven does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.
